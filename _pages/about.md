Hi! My name is Zixiao (Adrien) Zhao. I'm currently a PhD student in the School of Computer Science in the [University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en.html), under the supervision of Associate Professor [Jing Sun](https://profiles.auckland.ac.nz/jing-sun). My research topic is **Agentic program generation and automated validation for medical data analysis**, with a focus on automated generation, execution, and validation of machine learning workflows.

I speak Mandarin (mother tongue), English (C1), French (B2) and a little Spanish.

## Education Experience
I got my Bacholar of Engineering Degree in **Xidian University** (Xi'an University of Electronic Science and Technology, China), majored in Microelectronics. I then pursued my study in France and got my Engineer's Degree (equals to Master's Degree) of Imaging and Electronic System in **Télécom Saint-Etienne**. At the meantime, I also achieved a dual Master's Degree of Advanced Imaging and Material Appearance in **Université Jean Monnet**. Now, I'm studying in **University of Auckland**, New Zealand for my PhD.

## Work Experience
I used to work in the National University of Singapore (Suzhou) Research Institute as a Research Assistant, worked on an auto-machine learning platform, specifically for medical image processing domain (2020.10 - 2023.01).  
Then I joined a start-up company in Suzhou, named Bodhibrain, as a senior algorithm engineer, worked on a software of Fetal Brain Segmentation (2023.01 - 2023.10).
